Idea -> Design -> Build

**Idea:**
Book a consultation where we can flesh out your idea.
Focus on the essentials.
Figure out what is neccessary and what will waste you time and money.
Get realistic about the technical constraints and estimated costs.

This results in a list of technical requirements and a realistic understanding of what is might cost to build your website or application.

**Design:**
This is where your idea becomes fully realised.

Translate your business goals into features.
Perform user research and develop UI/UX for your customers.
Understand the flow of the software and test ideas before committing to a build.

This results in a written scope, interactive prototype and feature list for a developer.

**Build:**
I turn your idea into a reality. Results in a fully deployed website or application. You own the source code.

Developed in phases:
- Initial Build Testing
- Beta Build Delivery and Testing Phase 1 (your device)
- Beta Build Delivery and Testing Phase 2 (all platforms)
- Publishing

On-going maintaintence and hosting available.
Development is quoted per project.
Available for both independant and collaborative work.
