Task RPG

ABOUT

TaskRPG turns your to-do list into a quest log. No more boring chores! Gather action points to fight dangerous monsters, collect XP and find special items to help defeat your enemies. Only the most consistent adventurers will have a chance to face off against the gauntlet of bosses at the end.

  • Turn your to-do list into a quest log.
  • Earn AP (Action Points) by completing tasks.
  • Spend points to block attacks or strike down your foe.
